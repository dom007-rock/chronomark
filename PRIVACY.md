# Chronomark Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2026**

Chronomark is a Chrome extension that organizes your bookmarks into Year/Month
folders, detects duplicates, and makes your bookmark collection searchable.

## What Chronomark accesses

Chronomark uses exactly two Chrome permissions:

- **bookmarks** — to read, create, move, and organize your bookmarks, and to
  detect duplicate or misplaced ones.
- **storage** — to save the extension's own settings (the opt-in toggles) and
  small bookkeeping data (which bookmarks have already been filed) locally in
  your browser.

## What Chronomark does not do

- It does not transmit your bookmarks, browsing data, or any other
  information to any server, anywhere.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.
- It does not require or support any account or sign-in.
- It does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
- It does not use your data for advertising, credit, or lending purposes.
- It does not load or execute any remote code — everything it runs ships
  inside the extension package itself.

## Where your data lives

Everything Chronomark reads or writes stays on your own device, inside
Chrome's own bookmarks and local storage. Nothing ever leaves your computer.
